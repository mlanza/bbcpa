Pastor preached about a man with same-sex attraction who said I will never be happy, because he was torn between what the Bible said and his desires.
He struggled to see how he could make his way through life denying himself.
It wasn't long afterwards that he came back...
I thought he was going to say he gave in.
But he said he did a copious study of the scriptures and he said, "Pastor, I love God more than my sexuality and this is right." He decided he would move toward God and not his desire.

Earlier he had talked about what he commanded of Abraham: take your one and only son and kill him.  And many people struggled to make sense of it. The pastor worked to explain that God was just in his request and that, as we know his character, we trust that there was no unrighteousness in the request. And yet many people (outsiders) use it as a point to demonstrate a God they could not follow.
