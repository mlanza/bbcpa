# Devotional Project Guide

## Objective

Build a short devotional talk for the church deacons. Bullet-based outline suitable for presenting from.

## Execution Rules

### Phase 1: Spine First

- Establish the overall shape and flow before adding detail
- Connect the dots: each section must earn the next
- Keep it an outline — bullets, not prose
- Structural decisions get locked before content expands

### Phase 2: Meat

- Flesh out each section with supporting detail
- Tighten transitions
- Ensure each point lands concisely

### Constraints

- Short statements, not paragraphs
- Presentable from — clear enough to speak from without reading
- One screen's worth of cognitive load per major section
- No filler, no repetition
- The director's voice, not a generic devotional tone

## Source Material

- `sermon.md` — Sunday's sermon notes (two stories: man with same-sex attraction, Abraham)
- `why-jesus-often-teaches-indirectly.md` — on parables, the sower, and heart posture

## Structure Notes

The devotional has a throughline: **the disposition of the hearer determines everything.**

Two entry points converge on this:
1. Two stories that look troubling on the surface (sermon)
2. Jesus' teaching method and why it works this way (parables)

The payoff: a way to discern who genuinely trusts Scripture vs. who uses it as decoration.
