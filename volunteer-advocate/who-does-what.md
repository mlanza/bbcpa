# Volunteer Advocate — Who Does What

One-page summary of the fixes and the players responsible. Purpose: make every player visible and show where oversight sits.

## The Players

**Volunteer Advocate** — coordinator and system owner
- Pulls ministry staffing and slot data from Planning Center monthly.
- Publishes a twice-yearly cross-ministry staffing report (start date, headcount, required slots at minimum and normal staffing).
- Flags risk areas: below-minimum staffing, high call-off patterns, repeated fill-ins by the same people.
- Manages the First Call Queue; reviews recent gap recovery.
- Proposes adjustments: shift trades, buffer scheduling, role progression moves.
- Keeps coordination lightweight: one-screen ministry health snapshot, minimum new inputs.
- Surfaces pressure trends early; recommends scope limits when capacity isn't there.

**Leadership (pastoral)**
- Plans ministries and special events to staffing reality, not hope.
- Stops normalizing emergency asks as standard operations.
- Treats volunteer sustainability as a pastoral responsibility.

**Ministry Leaders**
- Logs call-offs, self-replacements, and fill-ins consistently.
- Maintains accurate role/slot definitions (shift duration, off-hours work, on-service vs off-service).
- Uses shift trades and progression moves before asking for stacked second jobs.
- Staffs special events early, separate from weekly coverage.

**Volunteers**
- Gives early notice when unavailable.
- Self-replaces or responds to First Call Queue requests to make up missed coverage.
- Treats fair follow-through as a shared commitment, not punishment.

**System / Planning Center** — the data layer that makes everything above possible
- Records key events: call-offs, self-replacements, filled gaps.
- Provides required metrics: current headcount, required slots (minimum vs normal), role durations.
- Represents First Call Queue status, directly or via workaround.
- Tracks role-level data per ministry: jobs/positions, loose descriptions, shift duration, on-service vs off-hours.

## Oversight Layer

- Single coordinator: the Volunteer Advocate surfaces pressure trends and recommends scope limits.
- Monthly: one-screen ministry health snapshot to leaders + proposed adjustments with owners.
- Twice-yearly: public staffing report and a congregation-facing summary reinforcing care for people, not just output.

## Operating Principle

Added dependencies must stay small, clear, and consistent. If the system feels heavy to operate, adoption fails.
