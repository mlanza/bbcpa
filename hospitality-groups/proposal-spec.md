# Hospitality Groups Proposal Packet Spec

Purpose: define the structure and required content for a single Markdown document that will be rendered to PDF for church leadership review and approval.

## Document Goal

Produce one leadership-ready packet that does two jobs:
1. Makes a clear decision request (approve pilot / do not approve).
2. Provides enough narrative and rationale to explain why this matters.

## Recommended Packet Order (single Markdown file)

1. Title Page (short)
2. Executive Summary (decision-first)
3. Proposal Body (operating model, pilot plan, support needed)
4. Narrative Rationale (your story + what is missing)
5. Optional Appendices (Planning Center workflow detail, draft communications, etc.)

## Narrative vs. Decision Division (important)

Use a deliberate split between conviction and approval mechanics:

- **Narrative Rationale section:** carry the emotional weight and story. This is where strong storytelling belongs.
- **Executive Summary + Proposal Body:** keep tone practical and decision-oriented (scope, ownership, support needed, timeline, metrics).

Rule of thumb:
- Let the narrative answer: **"Why this matters."**
- Let the decision sections answer: **"What exactly are we approving, and how will it run?"**

Do not flatten the story; make it vivid. But keep operational sections disciplined so leadership can approve quickly.

---

## 1) Title Page

Include:
- Document title: `Hospitality Groups Proposal (Pilot)`
- Church name
- Author
- Date
- Intended audience (e.g., pastors/elders/ministry leadership)

## 2) Executive Summary (keep to ~1 page)

### 2.1 Purpose
Provide 2–4 sentences:
- What you are proposing
- Why now
- What outcome you want

### 2.2 Decision Requested
State plainly:
- Exact decision needed
- Decision deadline
- Pilot start target

Example:
- "I am requesting approval to run a 90-day Hospitality Groups pilot beginning [date]."

### 2.3 Minimal Leadership Lift
Summarize in bullets:
- What leadership must do (small, specific)
- What leadership does **not** need to do

## 3) Proposal Body (implementation and governance)

### 3.1 Problem / Opportunity
Define current gap(s):
- What is missing in hospitality, connection, or follow-up
- Why existing process is insufficient

### 3.2 Proposed Solution
Describe Hospitality Groups in practical terms:
- Core activities
- Who participates
- How people flow through the process

### 3.3 Planning Center Operating Model
Document exactly how Planning Center will support execution:
- Which product(s) (People, Services, Workflows, Groups, etc.)
- Key workflow steps
- Automations and reminders
- Reporting/visibility

### 3.4 Ownership Model
Clarify responsibilities:
- What you own end-to-end
- What volunteers own
- What leadership owns (minimal)

### 3.5 Leadership Supports Required
List only what you need, with precision:
- Approval/covering
- Permissions/access in Planning Center
- Budget (if any)
- Public endorsement moments (if any)
- Escalation contact/person

### 3.6 Pilot Scope & Timeline
Define boundaries:
- Start/end date
- Included ministries/services/groups
- Exclusions for this pilot
- 30/60/90-day checkpoints

### 3.7 Success Metrics
Include 3–6 measurable outcomes:
- Participation/engagement indicators
- Follow-up completion rate
- Response time
- Volunteer sustainability indicators
- Qualitative signals (optional)

Add threshold language:
- "Pilot is considered successful if..."

### 3.8 Risks & Mitigations
For each risk, include mitigation + owner.
Examples:
- Volunteer fatigue
- Low adoption
- Data quality drift in Planning Center
- Role confusion

### 3.9 Recommendation at End of Body
Close with one paragraph:
- Why this is worth trying now
- Why this is feasible with low leadership overhead

## 4) Narrative Rationale (up to ~1 page)

Title suggestion:
- `Pastoral Rationale: Why Hospitality Groups, Why Now`

This section captures your story and burden:
- What you are seeing in church life
- What feels absent or underdeveloped
- Why this matters spiritually and relationally
- Why this model is an appropriate response

Guidance:
- Keep it personal, concrete, and non-abstract
- Avoid repeating operational details from Section 3
- Point back to the proposal ask at the end

## 5) Optional Appendices

Use only if needed:
- A: Planning Center workflow map (step-by-step)
- B: Draft communications/scripts
- C: Role descriptions and training outline
- D: Reporting template for monthly leadership update

## Content You Need to Provide (author checklist)

Fill these inputs before drafting final packet:

- [ ] Final decision ask (one sentence)
- [ ] Pilot duration and target start date
- [ ] Scope boundaries (what is in/out)
- [ ] Planning Center tools and exact workflow
- [ ] Named owners (you, volunteers, leadership contact)
- [ ] Required leadership supports (max 3–5 items)
- [ ] Metrics and success thresholds
- [ ] Top risks + mitigations
- [ ] Narrative rationale draft (your story)
- [ ] Any budget estimate (or explicit $0)

## Formatting Notes for Markdown -> PDF

- Use clear H1/H2/H3 headings for PDF table-of-contents support.
- Keep decision request near the top (first page).
- Prefer short paragraphs and bullet lists.
- Put long technical detail in appendices.
- Aim for 3 pages total before appendices.

## Suggested Output File Strategy

- Working draft: `hospitality-groups/concept.md`
- Structure spec (this file): `hospitality-groups/proposal-spec.md`
- Final proposal markdown: `hospitality-groups/proposal.md`
- Exported PDF: `hospitality-groups/proposal.pdf`
